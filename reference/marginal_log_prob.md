# Log density of one marginal, renormalized and masked outside its bounds

Log density of one marginal, renormalized and masked outside its bounds

## Usage

``` r
marginal_log_prob(m, x)
```

## Arguments

- m:

  A marginal from
  [`new_marginal()`](https://neuralsbi.pedrodelima.com/reference/new_marginal.md).

- x:

  Numeric vector of values.
